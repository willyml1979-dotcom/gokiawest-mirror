Federal Incentives: The iZEV Program

The federal government's Incentives for Zero-Emission Vehicles (iZEV) program offers point-of-sale rebates on eligible battery electric vehicles (BEVs), plug-in hybrid electric vehicles (PHEVs), and hydrogen fuel cell vehicles. As of the most recent program guidelines, eligible buyers can receive up to $5,000 on qualifying fully electric vehicles and up to $2,500 on qualifying plug-in hybrids, depending on the vehicle's base price and electric range.

To qualify for the iZEV rebate, the vehicle must be purchased or leased for personal or business use, and the Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) must fall below the program's price cap — currently set at $55,000 for base trim levels, with higher caps for vehicles with additional packages. Several Kia models, including the EV6 and EV9, have qualified under this program. Our team at Go Kia West applies the federal rebate directly at the point of sale, meaning you see the savings immediately rather than waiting for a tax return.

It is important to note that iZEV funding is allocated and can be exhausted. We recommend confirming current availability when you visit us, as program terms and vehicle eligibility can be updated by the federal government at any time.

British Columbia Provincial Incentives: CleanBC Go Electric

British Columbia offers one of the most generous provincial EV incentive programs in the country through the CleanBC Go Electric program, administered by the Province and delivered through the New Vehicles portion of the program. Eligible BC residents can receive up to $4,000 on the purchase or lease of a qualifying new battery electric vehicle, and up to $2,000 on a qualifying plug-in hybrid electric vehicle. These rebates are income-tested, meaning higher-income households may receive a reduced amount or may not qualify.

In addition to the standard rebate, BC also offers an enhanced rebate of up to $4,000 for low- and medium-income households through the Affordable EV Program, bringing total potential provincial savings even higher for qualifying buyers. Eligibility is determined based on household income as reported on your most recent tax return, and the application process is handled through the Province's online portal.

The provincial rebate can be stacked on top of the federal iZEV rebate, which means eligible buyers in British Columbia can potentially combine both programs for maximum savings. Go Kia West can help you understand how these programs interact and what documentation you may need to bring when you visit us.

Charging Infrastructure Rebates and Utility Incentives

Owning an electric or plug-in hybrid vehicle is only part of the equation — having a reliable home charging setup is just as important. BC Hydro and FortisBC both offer rebates to help customers offset the cost of purchasing and installing a Level 2 home EV charger. BC Hydro's EV charging rebate has offered up to $350 toward the cost of a qualified home charging station, while FortisBC has offered similar programs for customers in their service area. These programs are separate from the vehicle purchase incentives and are applied for directly through the utility provider.

For those who live in strata buildings or multi-unit residential properties, both the provincial government and some utilities have specific programs to help fund EV-ready infrastructure in shared parking areas. This is a growing area of support, as a significant portion of British Columbia's population lives in condominiums or apartments.

Public charging infrastructure in BC is also expanding rapidly, with a network of DC fast chargers and Level 2 stations available throughout the province via networks like FLO, ChargePoint, and BC Hydro's own EV fast-charging network. Our staff at Go Kia West can provide guidance on apps and networks that work best with your specific Kia EV or PHEV model.

Business and Fleet Incentives

Businesses and self-employed individuals in British Columbia may be eligible for additional incentives beyond what is available to individual consumers. The federal iZEV program does extend to business purchases, and businesses may also be able to claim accelerated capital cost allowance (CCA) deductions on zero-emission vehicles under federal tax rules, which can significantly reduce the net cost of adding EVs or PHEVs to a fleet.

The Province of BC has also supported fleet electrification through various programs, and eligible businesses should consult with their accountant or tax advisor to understand the full scope of deductions and credits available to them. Go Kia West works with a number of local businesses and fleet operators, and we are happy to connect you with resources that can help clarify the business case for switching to electric.

Whether you are purchasing a single vehicle for a small business or looking to electrify a larger fleet, the combination of purchase incentives, tax treatment, and lower operating costs can make a compelling financial argument for making the switch sooner rather than later.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I combine the federal iZEV rebate and the BC CleanBC Go Electric rebate on the same vehicle?

Yes, in most cases these two programs can be stacked, meaning you may be eligible to receive both the federal rebate and the provincial rebate on a single qualifying vehicle purchase or lease. The total combined savings depend on the specific vehicle, your income level, and the current program terms at the time of purchase. Our team at Go Kia West can help you confirm eligibility and walk through how both programs apply to your chosen model.

Which Kia models currently qualify for EV rebates in British Columbia?

Kia's lineup includes several models that have qualified for federal and provincial incentives, including the fully electric EV6 and EV9, as well as plug-in hybrid models like the Sportage PHEV and Niro PHEV. Eligibility depends on the specific trim level, MSRP, and the program guidelines in effect at the time of purchase. Because these programs are updated periodically, we recommend speaking directly with our sales team at Go Kia West to confirm which models and trims are currently eligible.

How is the rebate applied — do I get a cheque, or is it taken off the purchase price?

For the federal iZEV program, the rebate is applied directly at the point of sale by the dealership, so you see the savings immediately as a reduction in the vehicle's purchase price — you do not need to apply separately or wait for a government payment. The provincial CleanBC Go Electric rebate may require a separate application through the Province's online portal, depending on the program stream. We will explain the exact process for each applicable rebate when you visit us.

Do lease customers qualify for EV rebates, or are they only for buyers?

Both the federal iZEV program and the BC CleanBC Go Electric program extend to eligible leases, not just outright purchases. There are typically minimum lease term requirements — for example, the iZEV program has required a minimum lease term of 12 months. If you are considering leasing an electric or plug-in hybrid Kia, our team can confirm whether your intended lease structure qualifies for the available incentives.

Is there a rebate available for installing a home EV charger in British Columbia?

Yes, both BC Hydro and FortisBC have offered rebate programs to help offset the cost of purchasing and installing a Level 2 home EV charger. The amount and availability of these rebates can change, so we recommend visiting the BC Hydro and FortisBC websites directly, or asking our team for the most current information. Installing a Level 2 charger at home is highly recommended for daily convenience, and the rebates make it more affordable to do so.

What happens if program funding runs out before I take delivery of my vehicle?

Government EV incentive programs are funded with a set budget, and rebates are typically issued on a first-come, first-served basis until funds are exhausted. If funding is depleted before your vehicle is delivered, you may not receive the rebate, depending on the program's specific rules around reservation and delivery dates. This is one reason we encourage customers not to delay if they are serious about taking advantage of current incentives. Our team at Go Kia West monitors program status and will keep you informed throughout your purchase process.
