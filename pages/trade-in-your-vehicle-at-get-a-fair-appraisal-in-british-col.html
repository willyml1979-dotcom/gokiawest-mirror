How We Determine Your Trade-In Value

Vehicle appraisal is not guesswork. When you bring your vehicle to us, our trained appraisers conduct a thorough inspection that takes several factors into account: the year, make, model, and trim level of your vehicle; its current mileage; the overall condition of the exterior, interior, and mechanical components; any accident or service history; and current market demand in British Columbia for that type of vehicle.

We also reference industry-standard valuation tools and current wholesale market data to ensure our offer reflects real-world conditions. Vehicles that are in high demand, have lower mileage, or are in excellent condition will naturally receive stronger offers. We will walk you through the reasoning behind our appraisal so you understand exactly how we arrived at the number.

It is worth noting that trade-in values fluctuate with the market. Factors like fuel prices, seasonal demand, and inventory levels across the province can all influence what a vehicle is worth at any given time. Getting an appraisal sooner rather than later means you can take advantage of current conditions.

What to Bring When You Come In for an Appraisal

To make your appraisal appointment as efficient as possible, there are a few items you should bring with you. The most important is your vehicle itself — our appraisers need to physically inspect it to give you an accurate offer. Along with the vehicle, bring your vehicle registration, proof of insurance, and a valid government-issued ID.

If your vehicle has a lien on it — meaning you still owe money to a lender — bring your most recent loan statement or know your account number so we can obtain a payout figure. This is completely normal and does not prevent you from trading in. We will calculate the difference between your trade-in value and your remaining loan balance and factor that into your deal structure.

Any service records you have can also work in your favour. A documented maintenance history demonstrates that the vehicle has been well cared for, which can positively influence the appraisal. If you have made any upgrades or repairs recently, let our appraiser know so those can be considered as well.

How the Trade-In Process Works Step by Step

The process at Go Kia West is designed to be clear and low-pressure. It begins with your appraisal appointment, which typically takes between 30 and 60 minutes depending on the vehicle. Our appraiser will inspect the car, review any documentation you have brought, and then present you with a written offer. You are under no obligation to accept.

If you decide to move forward, the trade-in value is applied directly to the purchase price of your next vehicle. This reduces the amount you need to finance or pay out of pocket, and in British Columbia, it also reduces the taxable amount of your new vehicle purchase — meaning you only pay Provincial Sales Tax on the difference between the new vehicle price and your trade-in value, not on the full purchase price. This is a meaningful financial benefit that private sales do not offer.

Once the numbers are agreed upon, our finance team handles the paperwork, including settling any outstanding loan on your trade-in if applicable. From appraisal to driving away in your new vehicle, the entire process can often be completed in a single visit.

Tips for Getting the Most from Your Trade-In

A little preparation before your appraisal can make a real difference in the offer you receive. Start by giving your vehicle a thorough cleaning inside and out. A clean vehicle makes a better first impression and allows the appraiser to assess the condition accurately without surface dirt obscuring the paint or upholstery.

Address any minor issues that are inexpensive to fix, such as replacing burned-out bulbs or topping up fluids. However, be cautious about investing in major repairs before trading in — in many cases, the cost of a significant repair will not be fully recovered in the trade-in offer. Our team can advise you honestly on whether a repair is likely to improve your offer enough to justify the expense.

Finally, know your vehicle's history before you arrive. If there have been accidents, be upfront about them. Our appraisers will identify these through inspection or history reports regardless, and transparency helps build trust and keeps the process moving smoothly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I trade in a vehicle that I still owe money on?

Yes, absolutely. Having an outstanding loan on your vehicle does not disqualify it from being traded in. We will obtain the payout figure from your lender and factor it into the transaction. If your trade-in value exceeds what you owe, the difference is applied toward your new vehicle. If you owe more than the vehicle is worth — sometimes called being upside down or in negative equity — that remaining balance can often be rolled into your new financing, subject to lender approval. We will explain all of your options clearly before you make any decisions.

Do I have to buy a vehicle from Go Kia West to get an appraisal?

No. We are happy to appraise your vehicle without any obligation to purchase. That said, our appraisal offers are most valuable when applied toward a vehicle purchase with us, since you also benefit from the provincial tax savings on the difference. If you decide not to proceed with a purchase, the appraisal still gives you useful information about your vehicle's current market value.

How long does a trade-in appraisal take?

Most appraisals are completed within 30 to 60 minutes. The time can vary depending on the type of vehicle and its condition. We recommend booking an appointment in advance so we can dedicate proper time to your appraisal, though walk-ins are welcome when our schedule allows. Bringing all relevant documents with you will help keep things moving efficiently.

Will my trade-in value change if I wait a few weeks?

It is possible. Trade-in values are influenced by market conditions, including used vehicle supply and demand, fuel prices, and seasonal trends in British Columbia. Values can go up or down depending on these factors. Our written appraisal offer is typically valid for a defined period, which we will communicate to you clearly. If you are considering trading in, it is generally worthwhile to get an appraisal sooner so you have an accurate picture of where things stand.

What types of vehicles do you accept as trade-ins?

We accept a wide range of vehicles as trade-ins, including cars, trucks, SUVs, and vans from virtually any manufacturer. The vehicle does not need to be a Kia. We evaluate each vehicle on its own merits based on condition, mileage, and market demand. Even older vehicles or high-mileage vehicles may have trade-in value, so it is always worth bringing your vehicle in for an assessment.

How does trading in save me money on taxes in British Columbia?

In British Columbia, when you trade in a vehicle at a licensed dealership, the Provincial Sales Tax on your new vehicle purchase is calculated on the net amount after your trade-in value is deducted. For example, if you purchase a vehicle for $40,000 and your trade-in is valued at $15,000, you pay PST on $25,000 rather than the full $40,000. This tax benefit is exclusive to dealership trade-ins and is not available when selling privately and then purchasing separately. It can represent a significant saving depending on the value of your trade.
