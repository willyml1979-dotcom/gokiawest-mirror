How We Determine Your Trade-In Value

When you bring your vehicle in for an appraisal, our team evaluates it based on several key factors: the year, make, model, trim level, current mileage, overall mechanical condition, and the condition of the interior and exterior. We also take into account current market demand for your specific vehicle in British Columbia, which can vary depending on the season and regional trends.

We use industry-standard valuation tools alongside our own knowledge of the local used vehicle market to arrive at a number that reflects what your car is genuinely worth today. We encourage you to research your vehicle's estimated value ahead of time using tools like Canadian Black Book or Carfax Canada so you come in with realistic expectations and a basis for comparison.

It is important to understand that trade-in values differ from private sale values. A private sale may yield a higher dollar amount, but it comes with the time, effort, and risk of selling on your own. Trading in offers convenience, speed, and the ability to roll the transaction into a single, streamlined deal.

How to Prepare Your Vehicle for a Trade-In Appraisal

A little preparation before your appraisal appointment can make a meaningful difference in the value you receive. Start by giving your vehicle a thorough cleaning — both inside and out. Remove all personal belongings, vacuum the interior, and wash the exterior. A clean vehicle presents better and helps our appraisers see its true condition without distraction.

Gather any service records you have, including oil changes, tire rotations, brake work, or any major repairs. A documented maintenance history signals to us that the vehicle has been well cared for, which can positively influence the appraisal. If you have a second set of keys or original accessories such as floor mats or a cargo cover, bring those along as well.

Be honest about any known mechanical issues or accident history. We will identify these during our inspection regardless, and full transparency from the start leads to a smoother, faster process for everyone involved.

The Trade-In Process at Go Kia West: Step by Step

The trade-in process at Go Kia West is designed to be straightforward. It begins with either an online estimate or an in-person appraisal. You can start by submitting your vehicle details through our website to receive a preliminary estimate, or you can bring your vehicle directly to our lot in British Columbia for a hands-on evaluation by one of our trained appraisers.

Once we have completed the appraisal, we will present you with a written trade-in offer. This offer is typically valid for a set period, giving you time to consider it without pressure. If you decide to proceed, the trade-in value is applied directly to your vehicle purchase — either reducing the purchase price or serving as part of your down payment. Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to buy a vehicle from Go Kia West to trade in my car?

In most cases, we do require that your trade-in be part of a vehicle purchase transaction with us. However, we encourage you to come in for an appraisal so we can discuss your specific situation. Our goal is to make the process work for you wherever possible.

Can I trade in a vehicle that I still owe money on?

Yes, you can trade in a vehicle that has an outstanding loan balance. We will obtain a payout figure from your lender and factor that into the transaction. If your vehicle is worth more than what you owe, the difference is positive equity and is applied toward your new purchase. If you owe more than the vehicle is worth — sometimes called being 'underwater' or having negative equity — the remaining balance can often be rolled into your new financing, though this will affect your monthly payments. We will walk you through exactly how this works before you commit to anything.

How long does a trade-in appraisal take?

An in-person appraisal at our dealership typically takes between 30 and 60 minutes, depending on the vehicle. Our appraisers will inspect the exterior, interior, and mechanical condition of the vehicle and review any relevant documentation you bring. We aim to have a written offer ready for you the same day.

Does the trade-in value I receive online match what I will get in person?

Online estimates are a useful starting point, but they are based on the information you provide and general market data. The final in-person appraisal may differ once our team has had a chance to physically inspect the vehicle. Factors like undisclosed damage, higher-than-reported mileage, or mechanical issues can affect the final offer. We recommend treating the online estimate as a general guide rather than a guaranteed figure.

What vehicles do you accept as trade-ins?

We accept trade-ins of virtually any make, model, or year — not just Kia vehicles. Whether you are driving a domestic brand, a European import, or another Asian make, we are happy to evaluate it. The condition and market demand for your specific vehicle will influence the offer we make, but we do not restrict trade-ins to any particular brand.

How does trading in a vehicle affect my taxes in British Columbia?

In British Columbia, when you trade in a vehicle at a dealership, the trade-in value is deducted from the purchase price of the new vehicle before Provincial Sales Tax (PST) is calculated. This means you only pay PST on the difference between the new vehicle's price and your trade-in value, which can result in meaningful tax savings compared to buying and selling privately. We recommend consulting with your accountant or a tax professional for advice specific to your situation, but this is one of the financial advantages of trading in through a licensed dealership.
