Kia Warranty Coverage

New Kia vehicles come with one of the most comprehensive warranty packages in the industry. The standard coverage includes a 5-year/100,000 km comprehensive limited warranty, a 5-year/100,000 km powertrain warranty, and a 5-year unlimited kilometre roadside assistance program. Kia's electric and hybrid vehicles carry additional coverage for their high-voltage battery systems — typically 8 years or 160,000 km depending on the model.

It's important to understand what your warranty does and does not cover. The comprehensive warranty protects against defects in materials and workmanship across most vehicle components. Wear items such as brake pads, wiper blades, and tires are generally not included, as they are considered normal wear and tear. Our service advisors can walk you through your specific warranty documents and answer any questions about what applies to your vehicle.

Warranty work is performed at no cost to you when the repair falls within the coverage terms. As an authorized Kia dealership serving British Columbia, Go Kia West is fully authorized to perform all warranty repairs using genuine parts and approved procedures. If your vehicle was purchased elsewhere, you are still welcome to bring it to us for warranty service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my Kia warranty cover maintenance services like oil changes?

No, Kia's standard warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship — it does not cover routine maintenance items such as oil changes, filter replacements, tire rotations, or brake pad wear. These are the owner's responsibility and should be performed according to the intervals outlined in your owner's manual. However, if you have a prepaid maintenance plan, those services may be included depending on the plan you selected.

Can I bring my Kia to Go Kia West for warranty work if I bought it at another dealership?

Yes, absolutely. As an authorized Kia dealership in British Columbia, we are fully qualified to perform warranty repairs on any Kia vehicle, regardless of where it was originally purchased. Simply bring your vehicle in and we'll verify the warranty coverage using your VIN and service records. There is no cost to you for repairs that fall within the warranty terms.

What happens if my vehicle breaks down and I need roadside assistance?

New Kia vehicles come with 5-year unlimited kilometre roadside assistance coverage. This program provides help for situations such as towing, flat tires, battery boost, lockout service, and fuel delivery. You can access Kia Roadside Assistance by calling the number listed in your owner's manual or on the Kia Canada website. Our service team can also help you navigate the process if you're unsure how to make a claim.

How often does my Kia need to be serviced?

Kia recommends service intervals based on kilometres driven, typically starting around every 8,000 km for oil changes and more comprehensive inspections at longer intervals. The exact schedule depends on your specific model and how you drive — for example, vehicles used in severe conditions such as frequent short trips, extreme temperatures, or heavy towing may require more frequent service. Your owner's manual contains the full maintenance schedule, and our service advisors are happy to review it with you.
